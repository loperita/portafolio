Mobile app · own product

Knife Poker — Texas Hold'em analysis

KotlinJetpack ComposeMaterial 3KtorFlutter 3Dart 3Monte Carlo116 tests
Situation
Poker is won with math-based decisions, not gut feeling. Working out a hand's real odds by hand and weighing them against the money on the table is slow and complex; existing tools are desktop-only and in English.
Task
Build a poker engine end to end (evaluate hands, calculate odds and decide) plus a smooth mobile interface, at real product quality. Then take the app to native Android without losing anything that already worked.
Action
Version 1 (Flutter): pure Dart engine decoupled from the UI; equity via Monte Carlo + exact enumeration; decision calculator (pot odds, EV, implied odds); computation in an isolate so the UI never freezes; ~56 tests. Version 2 (Kotlin): native rewrite with Jetpack Compose and Material 3; the engine moved to a pure Kotlin module, separate from Android, that is tested in seconds; releases follow SemVer with a changelog.
Result
An ODDS calculator that instantly returns the probability of winning and a clear decision (CALL / FOLD). The Kotlin version matched everything in the first one and went further: preflop range charts (4 player profiles × 6 positions), poker news that can be read offline and a faster animated splash screen, with 116 tests backing the engine and the app. Next up: communities and events, and a Play Store release.
